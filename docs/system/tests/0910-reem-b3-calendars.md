## Notes

Spec `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-secretary-reem-b3-calendar-read-design.md`, plan `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-05-reem-b3-calendar-read.md`. `gcal` reuses the Google OAuth client (`GOOGLE_CLIENT_ID`, `GOOGLE_CLIENT_SECRET`) and needs its own redirect `/api/connect/gcal/callback` and the two calendar scopes added in Google's console; `mscal` needs `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_ID` and `MICROSOFT_CLIENT_SECRET` (`docs/system/variables.md`). Without a client the provider is not configured and the calendar card offers no link.
