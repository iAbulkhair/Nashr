Decisions the founder delegated (2026-10-20), recorded here and in the pull request: away = no visible app tab of that login for 5 minutes (`AWAY_MINUTES`), heartbeat every 2 minutes (`BEAT_SECONDS`); only waiting and stuck push, done and working never; one push per workspace, agent and state per 30 minutes; at most 10 devices per workspace, the oldest dropped; a failure shows stuck for 3 days; a render still working after 30 minutes shows stuck; an agent with no history is quiet (no badge); Reem is not listed (she keeps the owner's own to-dos); owners and members may subscribe, MCP tokens and founder views may not; the push service keeps a push 6 hours; no third-party gateway and no new library (the VAPID token is signed with Node's `crypto`, the push has no body).
