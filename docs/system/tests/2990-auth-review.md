# Coverage: fixes from the authentication review (rules 2990-2992)

Status on 2026-10-22 on branch `claude/thakeen-nashr-review-tests-292d14`. A reviewer reported three authentication defects; each was checked against the code, found real, and fixed here with a test. Rules 2990 and 2992 are latent while `MULTI_ORG` is off (src/lib/multi-org.ts): a `p_` person cannot own a second workspace or join one yet. Rule 2991 was live. Release step: none. No stored shape changes: rule 2991 adds two rate buckets (`rate:pwchange:{login}`, `rate:acctdelete:{login}`, built by `accountKey.passwordChangeBucket` and `accountKey.accountDeleteBucket` in src/lib/keys.ts), and rules 2990 and 2992 change only which records are read and revoked.

| 2990 | One birth for a login (`loginBornAt`, src/lib/own-password.ts), shared by sign-in (`bornAt` in src/lib/login-finish.ts, used by `finishLogin` and POST /api/auth/login/mfa) and the session's own settings (`actingLogin`: two-step setup, passkeys through `freshProof`, address change, transfer). The birth is a legacy owner's account `createdAt`, or a legacy member's record `acceptedAt`, or otherwise the person's own `createdAt`. It is never the account or membership the login acts in. Before, a `p_` owner who turned on two-step sign-in and then created a second workspace could sign in with a password alone when `getLogin` picked the younger account. A `p_` person acting in a workspace joined after the enrolment passed the `already_on` check, and confirm replaced the secret without the current code. The same applied to a passkey add and an address change. | 2991 | PATCH /api/account (password change) and DELETE /api/account (deletion) compare the current password behind their own wrong-try buckets. These are `pwchange:{login}` and `acctdelete:{login}`, 10 per hour, with `allow` before the compare and `giveBack` after a right one. Over the limit, the answer is 429 `slow_down` with Retry-After, the body POST /api/account/2fa gives. Before, a stolen session cookie could guess here without limit | `tests/auth-review-2990.test.ts` (rule 2991) | none |
| 2992 | A password reset (POST /api/auth/reset) and a password change (PATCH /api/account) revoke in every account the person owns. An owner reset stamps `teamSessionsAfter` and revokes MCP tokens and grants, linked calendars, and the feed and booking links of the owner and every roster. Each account is handled under its own user lock inside the person lock, and an account whose owner changed is skipped. A member reset, and a change made while acting as a member, also revoke the MCP tokens of any account the person owns (a member reset revokes its calendars too).
