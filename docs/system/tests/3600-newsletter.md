# Coverage: newsletter sign-up, double opt-in and one-click unsubscribe (rules 3600-3609)

Status on 2026-10-23, branch `claude/sup-newsletter`. Thakeen has a working newsletter list: a visitor leaves an address in the footer or on the blog, gets one confirm mail, and is a subscriber only after pressing the confirm button. Every mail of the list is to carry a one-click unsubscribe link. The console lists the addresses with their status and exports them as CSV. Nothing is sent to subscribers from this change (no campaign tool); Supastarter's form saved nothing, this one does. Release step: expand only. New keys (`newsletter:*`), nothing old is read or changed, rollback leaves them in place. Rule 3609 is spare. All rows are pinned by `tests/newsletter-3600.test.ts` unless a cell says otherwise.
