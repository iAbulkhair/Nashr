An adversarial review of the first version found that hoisting the data read out of the refunding `try` lost the credits on a failed read after the charge (fixed, and pinned by the refund test), and that the reviewer and judge still lacked the memory files. Each rule was checked by reverting its fix and watching its test fail (ten reverts). The golden set moved to version 5 because the judge's inputs and scoring changed; a before/after run waits until Claude is switched back on in production.

Rules 90-91 were added on 2026-09-28 with the ad codes. Building them surfaced a real defect: a code's digits made the "contains a number or price" pattern track Nashr's own ads instead of the wording, which would have taught Noor a false lesson the more owners used codes.

Rules 87-89 were added on 2026-09-28 with the Meta Ads and Snapchat Ads readers, which turn the CSV import's one-off upload into a daily read of the owner's own ad accounts.

Rules 82-84 were added on 2026-09-28 with the campaign CSV import, the small-business form of a closed loop: until the ad connectors report results, an export the owner uploads is how Noor learns what worked.

Rules 77-80 were added on 2026-09-28 with the brand gate, the MCP brand tools and the launch journey. Until then the banned words reached the model as a prompt line, the reviewer as a checklist item and the judge as a score, and nothing in code stopped a draft containing one from being approved, auto-approved or published.

Rule 64 was added on 2026-09-22 with the honest-claims change. Until then the claims test read `content/blog` only, while the homepage log showed invented results (a revenue figure, a customer count, an acceptance rate) and the plan cards sold features that are not built. The sweep was checked by running it against main before the fix: every one of its 21 patterns matched at least once (78 hits), and none match after it. The review of 27 Sep widened the patterns to the Arabic wordings and the other word forms of the same removed claims. Four more were added the same day for the follow-ups outside that change (the free-tool pages' daily-automation line, the chat prompt's "no auto-publishing" on trial, the inspiration cards' unsourced results, and the unsourced superlatives in the season and industry prompts); on main before the fix they matched 9 lines, and none after it. The affiliate terms, the first-100-brands offer and the early-partner discount are owner decisions and are not in the list.
