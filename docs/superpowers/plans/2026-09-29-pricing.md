# Thakeen plan pricing — implementation plan

Design: `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-29-pricing-design.md`. Each phase ships as its own PR, runs `npm run check` and
`npx next build`, updates `docs/system/*` in the same commit, and adds or updates a test in `tests/` for every rule it
touches. No account pays yet, so no phase needs a customer migration; test and founder accounts are moved by the
founder console.

## Phase 1 — Plans, credits, floor · built 2026-09-30 (branch `claude/pricing-phase-1`), phase 4 included

Started on the founder's "ابدأ المرحلة 1 من التسعير". It deploys after the blocking owner items in
`docs/Owner_Tasks_AR.md` section ح3: the 299 / 499 / 999 pay links (`PAY_LINK_STARTER`, `PAY_LINK_PRO`,
`PAY_LINK_BUSINESS`; `PAY_LINK_AGENCY` removed), a deal for every agency or old enterprise account before the monthly
renewal, and a message to everyone who was told 149 / 399 / 799 (owner rule #191). What was built, with the
corrections to the list this section used to hold:

- **One plan table, `src/lib/pricing.ts`**, import-free (rule 249): `Plan` = trial, starter, pro, business, agency;
  `PLAN_PRICE_SAR` 299 / 499 / 999, `PLAN_CREDITS` trial 50, starter 300, pro 700, business 1,500, `PLAN_SEATS` 3 / 1 /
  3 / 7, keyed by the fixed plans only so `PLAN_CREDITS[u.plan]` does not compile. `auth.ts` re-exports it;
  `admin-stats.ts` no longer holds a price. The seat table keeps the name `PLAN_SEATS` (tests, docs and the teams spec
  use it), not `SEAT_LIMIT`. `CHAT_ALLOWANCE` is not added: an unenforced number in code waits for phase 2 (added and enforced there on 2026-10-01).
- **Enterprise folded into Agency**: a stored `enterprise` reads as `agency` (`planFromStore` in `getUser`) and is
  written back as `agency` on the next save; any other unknown stored plan reads as `trial` and is never renewed.
- **Agency deal**: `User.deal = {credits, priceSar, setAt, setBy}`, set only in the founder console
  (`POST /api/admin/plan`), validated in code: whole numbers, at least 999 SAR and 1,500 credits and never cheaper per
  credit than Business (price × 1,500 ≥ credits × 999, rules 243-244). Without a valid deal an Agency account gets
  Business's 1,500 credits and 7 seats and counts 999 SAR in MRR. A deal's seats go through `User.seats`. The spec's
  "about 2,000 SAR a month" minimum is sales guidance and is not enforced (founder decision 2026-09-29).
- **Renewal and MRR** read `allowanceOf` and `priceOf`; MRR and the paying count skip suspended accounts.
- **Task costs stay in `src/lib/tasks.ts`** (the import-free catalogue, not `brand.ts`): `weekly_plan` 1 → 2,
  `followup_seq` 3 → 4, and `weekly_report` 2 → 3 (founder decision 2026-09-29: the routine runs it on a cold cache,
  where 2 credits cost $0.0918 each). The draft types the chat may draft (`DRAFT_TYPES`) are checked in code (rule 250).
- **The floor test, `tests/pricing-floor.test.ts` (rule 241)**, reads the per-type token estimates from a server-only
  `src/lib/task-costs.ts` (not a table next to `TASK_COST`: `tasks.ts` must stay import-free) and prices them with
  `costUsd`. The ceiling is $0.07104 per credit (40% of Business's $0.1776). Cron-driven types (the Sunday plan and every
  routine's type) are checked on a cold cache, the rest on a hit. The one-item chat draft ($0.0716 for 1 credit) is a
  named exemption with a cap at today's estimate. Media models are checked too.
- **Brands: unlimited on every plan.** `BRAND_LIMIT` is deleted. The per-hour creation rate limit the old line said to
  keep did not exist; it was added: 10 an hour and 30 a day per account (`brandAddRefusal`), with the brand list
  written under a lock (rule 245). No hidden cap.
- **Phase 4 (brand guardrails) pulled into this phase**: onboarding a second or later brand from its URL costs 9
  credits (`ONBOARD_EXTRA_BRAND_COST`; the manual brand form is free), shown before confirm, 402 before any stream when
  unaffordable, refunded when the brand file is not saved, never charged twice (the charge is held on the account record,
  `User.onboardPaid`, in the same save as the debit; the daily cron refunds a hold no run used for a day); the first
  brand stays free; mock mode charges too; admins may spend it like the owner (rule 246). The Sunday plan is on by
  default only for the default brand; added brands opt in, `saveSchedule` stores only explicit fields, and an added
  brand's stored `weeklyPlan` counts only with the stamp the owner's switch writes (rule 247; the default is in `getSchedule` in `src/lib/schedule.ts`,
  which `runWeekly` reads, not in `cron-batch.ts`, which only fans the job out).
- **Public numbers from the table (part of phase 7 pulled in)**: the pricing cards (Starter, Pro, Business, Agency
  "Contact us" with a 1,500-credit floor), the credits table, the FAQ, help, industry, comparison and landing copy,
  the billing page (one pay link per self-serve tier, found by plan id) and the in-app plan labels render from
  `pricing.ts` and `TASK_COST` (rule 248). The Arabic name of the custom tier is «الوكالات» everywhere; the site nav item
  «المؤسسات» / "Enterprise" became «الوكالات» / "Agencies", while the `/enterprise` route and its title stay. The VAT
  sentence is unchanged, word for word, until the accountant answers. Outputs instead of credits, per-tier human anchors,
  the trial length and the launch offer stay in phases 3 and 7.
- `docs/system/costs.md` sections 3 and 4 rewritten for the new tiers; `tests.md` rules 241-250.

## Phase 2 — Chat allowance and model · allowance built 2026-10-01; the model switch still open

Started on the founder's «نعم قم بالاصلاحات الخمسة في توصيتك» (the five fixes: the chat allowance, overflow on
credits, hard per-message guards, showing the customer their consumption, and pricing the overflow at the 60% floor).
What was built, with the corrections to the list this section used to hold (rules 308-316 in `docs/system/tests.md`):

- **The allowance, `CHAT_ALLOWANCE` in `src/lib/pricing.ts`** (trial 30 for the whole trial, 250 / 650 / 1,300 a
  month; Agency gets Business's 1,300 unless the founder sets `User.chatAllowance`, 0..100,000, from the console). Not
  in `rate.ts`: the counter lives with the month's usage, `usage:{email}:{YYYY-MM}:chat_msgs` (so `usageMonthKeys` and
  `wipeAccount` cover it), and a trial's in `chatm:{email}:trial`, counted once. `src/lib/chat-allowance.ts` counts a
  message per ACCOUNT with INCRBY before `chat()` saves it, gives it back when the turn failed or never reached the
  model (a workspace too large for one message included), and is called by web chat and MCP `ask_noor` alike. The hourly and daily limits stay as a burst limit in
  front of it.
- **When it runs out: paused, or on credits if the owner says so** (founder decision 2026-10-01, refining spec section 6
  rule 1, whose "or a top-up" waits for phase 6). Chat pauses until the 1st (UTC), 402 `chat_allowance`, unless the
  owner of a paid plan turned "continue on credits" on (`POST /api/billing/chat-overflow`, owner only, never on a
  trial). Then an extra message goes ahead only when the balance is at least `chatOverflowHold()` (5 credits, the most
  one message can cost; 402 `chat_credits` below that). Nothing is held or taken before it, so the turn's own tools
  can spend the whole balance. After the reply it is billed its metered model cost at the floor rate
  (`maxCostPerCreditUsd`, the 60% margin on the cheapest credit), at most 5 credits and at most what the balance still
  holds, the remainder under one credit carried on the account (`User.chatCarryMicro`, `settleChatOverflow`), and the
  credits billed counted in `usage:{email}:{YYYY-MM}:chat_cred`. A failed message is not billed. (The first build
  took the 5 credits before the turn and gave back what it did not use; the review of 2026-10-01 removed that hold.)
- **Hard per-message guards in code** (founder decision 2026-10-01): one message may cost at most $0.35 in model calls
  (`CHAT_TURN_MAX_USD`); each call's `max_tokens` is what is left of it after the turn's cost so far and an upper
  estimate of the call's input (`src/lib/chat-budget.ts`: 3 characters a token; the cached prefix priced as a cache
  write on the first call and as a cache read on later ones), and the loop stops with a plain reply when fewer than
  2,048 output tokens would be left. A call the model ended at `max_tokens` is never acted on: its tool calls do not
  run, and its text is kept with a note. The history guard sends the model the newest 16 messages within 24,000
  characters, each cut to 6,000 (a thread that starts with the first-run onboarding's messages kept as it is), then
  drops the oldest until the first call has at least 4,000 output tokens (`fitFirstCall`); a workspace too large even
  for the newest message alone gets a reply that says so, and the message is not counted.
- **MCP gates**: `ask_noor` trims and caps the message at 4,000 characters, refuses an empty one, and goes through the
  same burst limit, allowance and settle, with a plain refusal sentence; a message that continued on credits ends with
  how many credits it was billed.
- **What the owner sees**: the messages-left line under the chat box, the refused message put back with the reason
  and the limit dialog (`chat`, `chat_credits`), the billing page's chat card with the switch, the profile's chat row
  and runway lines (`src/lib/usage-view.ts`), with the credits chat was billed this month, paused for a low balance,
  and chat off for an allowance of 0; every number from the status and `pricing.ts`, in Arabic and English. The public
  copy no longer calls chat free or unlimited, and no copy says credits are held or come back, or that a message
  usually costs under one credit: that is not measured (an Opus turn is estimated near $0.08, above one credit's
  $0.071 at the floor rate), and `tests/content-claims.test.ts` bans it until it is.
- **Founder console**: a Chat column (the raw count / allowance, the override, messages on credits and the credits
  they were billed, cost per message over every message sent), the
  override from a prompt (400 `bad_chat` for anything but 0..100,000; dropped with the deal like the seats), the
  detail pane, and a `chat_allowance` column in the users CSV.
- **Still open: the model.** Conversation turns stay on `claude-opus-5` (`CHAT_MODEL` in `src/lib/chat.ts`). They move
  to `claude-sonnet-5` only after the golden eval and a Gulf-dialect check pass (results recorded in the founder
  console quality tab). `docs/system/costs.md` section 4.1 shows why it matters: at the $0.35 ceiling a full Starter
  allowance costs more than Starter earns.
