# Voice notes in the chat composer (speech to text)

Date: 2026-10-23. Branch `claude/sup-voice-stt` (local, not pushed). Rules 3580-3599 (`docs/system/tests/3580-voice-stt.md`), flows section 122, permissions row 31c. The founder said yes to a "Speech-to-text model slot" (supastarter's AI module has a whisper-1 slot; read for reference only).

## What it does

A microphone button beside Send in the chat composer. Press to record, press again to stop (or Cancel / Escape to throw it away). At most two minutes. The note goes to `POST /api/chat/voice`, comes back as text, and is added to the message box. The person reads it, fixes it and sends it like typed text. Nothing is sent by itself, so no agent ever acts on speech nobody reviewed.

Off by default: `VOICE_STT_PROVIDER` unset (or its key missing) hides the button and the route answers 404.

### Pick: ElevenLabs Scribe v2

It has the only evidence of good Saudi dialect plus English code-switching, the lowest price per minute of the three, accepts every container a browser records (including Firefox's Ogg, which OpenAI does not take), and reports the billed length, so we charge on the provider's own figure. The cost is privacy: by default ElevenLabs may train on submitted audio, and zero retention is enterprise only.

Founder actions before turning it on:
1. In the ElevenLabs workspace that owns the key, turn off "Improve the models for everyone" (Data use settings). Without that, customers' voice notes may be used for training.
2. Create the key (speech-to-text permission only, if ElevenLabs offers scoped keys), set `ELEVENLABS_API_KEY` and then `VOICE_STT_PROVIDER=elevenlabs` on the host. Unset either to turn it off.
3. Add ElevenLabs to the sub-processor list in the privacy policy before it goes live (audio leaves for a US/EU host; no provider offers a Saudi region).

The seam (`src/lib/voice/transcribe.ts` `PROVIDERS`, `src/lib/voice/config.ts` rates and key names) takes a second adapter without touching the route: OpenAI `gpt-transcribe` is the privacy-stronger alternative (no training by default) if the founder prefers it, at roughly twice the price and with unmeasured Gulf quality.

## Credits (for the founder to confirm)

`VOICE_CREDITS_PER_MINUTE = 1` in `src/lib/pricing.ts`, billed per started minute, at least 1, at most 2 a note.

Derived the way other costs are: the smallest whole number of credits whose ceiling cost covers the provider's cost at the 60 % margin floor, `ceil(usdPerMinute / maxCostPerCreditUsd())`. With Scribe v2 at $0.22 an hour, a minute costs $0.00367; `maxCostPerCreditUsd()` is about $0.071 (40 % of the cheapest self-serve credit, the Business plan's, so the 60 % margin floor holds), so the result is 1. A credit covers about 19 minutes of Scribe at that floor, so 1 a minute is far above the floor; it cannot go lower without fractional credits. `tests/voice-stt.test.ts` fails if a provider rate rises past what the constant covers. The provider's cost is also added to the account's media cost counter, so the console's per-client cost includes it.

Open for the founder: keep 1 credit a minute, or make short notes free (would need a new rule; a free path needs its own abuse cap).

## Money order

1. Before the call: the hold is charged with `charge` (the ledger lock, the open agent's monthly cap, in the brand scope `sc(u)`, and the spending member from the session, `person: personOf(u)`, so the owner's monthly cap for that member applies and a member over it is refused as "no credits" before the provider is called; refunds lower that member's counter): the container's length rounded up to minutes, or 2 minutes when the container does not say (MediaRecorder's WebM often does not).
2. Provider failure, timeout or a reply that is not a transcript: the whole hold is refunded; the customer sees "busy" or "failed" and "no credits were used".
3. No speech: refunded, 422.
4. Success: billed on the provider's `audio_duration_secs` (else ours, else the hold), the rest refunded in the same request.
5. A note the provider says ran more than 5 s past two minutes (a file whose container hid its length, sent around the composer): the hold is kept and no text is returned.

## Safety

- Session first, role `chat`, 30 notes an hour and 120 a day per login (`src/lib/rate.ts`), byte cap 2.5 MB (two minutes of Opus at 32 kbit/s is about 0.5 MB), length cap from the container before charging and from the provider after.
- Audio type by magic numbers only (`src/lib/voice/audio.ts`): WebM (EBML DocType "webm", so Matroska video is refused), Ogg, MP4, WAV. The type sent to the provider comes from what we sniffed.
- The provider host is a constant; plain `fetch`, `redirect: "error"`, the key only in a header. Errors become codes; the log line holds the code and the HTTP status, never the body, the key or the audio.
- Storage choice: nothing is stored. The audio lives in the request's memory and is dropped after the call; the transcript is returned, never saved. There was no reason in the code to keep either.
- The transcript is untrusted text: control and direction characters removed, cut to 4,000 characters, and placed in the composer for review. It reaches a model only when the person sends it, through the normal chat route and its checks.
- `Permissions-Policy` now allows the microphone for this origin only (`microphone=(self)`); it was off for every page.
