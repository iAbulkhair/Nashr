## 2. Nashr's version — same mechanisms, Gulf channels

### 2.1 Founder-led launch (you)
- Arabic-first X thread + LinkedIn post + a 60–90s launch video (Arabic VO, English subs). Structure that worked for them: pain number → "we built X" → 6 arrow bullets of what it ships → proof → link. Drafts in §4.
- Launch on a Sunday 9am KSA (their Monday 8am PT). Line up 15–20 friendly accounts (founders, Salla/Zid merchants, marketers) to reply/quote in the first hour.
- Re-launch every feature: "Noor now imports your Salla products", "Noor MCP for Claude".

### 2.2 Programmatic SEO in Arabic (nobody has this)

Free without account (5/day), then "نور تسويها لك كل يوم" → signup.
- **Guides** (`/ar/blog/…`): "تسويق العيادات: الدليل الكامل 2026", "تسويق متاجر سلة", "إعلانات سناب شات", "تسويق العقار", "الجمعة البيضاء"… one per niche × season. Same template, daily.
- **Industry pages** (`/ar/for/…`): 7 verticals.
- **Comparisons** (`/ar/vs/…`): Nashr vs Enrich Labs, vs ChatGPT, vs Jasper, vs agency, vs Hootsuite/Buffer. Plus "بدائل X" lists.
- **Benchmark + definitional page in Arabic**: "ما هو وكيل التسويق بالذكاء الاصطناعي" — be the page Arabic LLM answers cite.

### 2.3 Partners paid on outcomes
- **Affiliates** 25% recurring, 60-day cookie: Gulf marketing agencies, freelancers on مستقل/بحر, YouTube/TikTok marketing educators, Salla/Zid consultants. Manual payouts via bank transfer until we add Rewardful.
- **Newsletter sponsorships**: Gulf/Arabic business & AI newsletters (Wamda, MAGNiTT, Arab startup/AI newsletters on Substack, LinkedIn newsletters by Saudi marketers). Also the same US AI newsletters if we chase expat/agency buyers (Superhuman, Ben's Bites, TAAFT) — but Gulf first.
- **Communities**: Salla/Zid merchant Telegram & WhatsApp groups, X spaces on التجارة الإلكترونية, Monsha'at and Misk programs, LEAP/GITEX side events.

### 2.4 In-product loops (built)
- Referral +50/+50 ✔, founder nudge email ✔ (needs Resend key), daily brief ✔.
- Add: "share Noor's output" — every approved post gets a "Made with Nashr" share card option (later).

### 2.5 Distribution inside platforms
- Salla + Zid app stores (playbook §5–6) — the single biggest lever in KSA.
- **Nashr MCP / Claude connector**: expose Noor as an MCP so Claude/ChatGPT users can run her. Copy their move exactly; list on claude.com connectors.
- AI directories (Arabic + global): TAAFT, Futurepedia, AIAXIO, Product Hunt, G2, Capterra; Arabic: "أدوات الذكاء الاصطناعي" directories, ArabHardware/Tech communities.

### 2.6 Trial & lifecycle
- Test **3-day trial + outcome promise** vs current 7-day/50 credits. Their data says urgency wins.
- Lifecycle: T+10min founder nudge (built), day 1 "first win" (show the National Day post she wrote), day 2 "trial ends tomorrow", day 3 "last day + WhatsApp me", day 7 win-back. All from your name, plain text.
