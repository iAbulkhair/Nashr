# Editorial review playbook

## Principles
- Correctness before polish: facts, compliance, language policy and broken format first, style last.
- Edit the minimum. Keep language, dialect, intent, structure, length and voice. A pass is a valid outcome; never invent faults.
- AI texture counts only as a cluster (3+ tells in one item). One tell alone is not a fault.
- Replace, never just delete. Never add an idea, fact or example the draft lacked. Leave specific details and accurate local dialect alone.

## Clarity and structure
- One idea per piece, one ask per message.
- Hook in line one: a reader problem, a result or a concrete detail. Not a greeting, company history or "هل تساءلت يوماً".
- Specific over vague: "خدمة مميزة" fails "so what?". Use a brand-file fact or cut the line. Every feature carries its benefit.
- CTA present, single (if two, keep the one that fits the goal), concrete: "اطلب من الرابط", not "تواصل معنا اليوم".
- Scannable: short paragraphs, point in the first third, sentences under about 25 words.

## AI-sounding text to remove
English:
- Filler openers and signposting ("In today's world", "Let's dive in", "Here's the thing"): start with the point.
- delve, leverage, robust, seamless, crucial, landscape, unlock: a plain word or the concrete fact.
- Triads and "not just X, it's Y": one direct claim.
- Hedging ("it's important to note", "could potentially"): state it or cut it.
- Em dashes: comma, colon or period. Generic sign-offs ("the future looks bright", "I hope this helps"), decorative emoji, stacked "!": delete.
Arabic:
- Translated English: "يلعب دوراً", "قم بزيارة" (write "زر"), "تم إطلاق" (write "أطلقنا"), "في عالم اليوم". Use a direct verb.
- Stiff connectors in dialect posts: "علاوة على ذلك", "في الختام", "حيث", "لم/لن". Use "ما", "عشان", or none. Fine in articles and documents.
- Inflation: "لا شك أن", "نقلة نوعية", "حلول مبتكرة", repeated "نحن نؤمن". Say what the product does.
- Rhyming padding and triads ("اكتشف. جرّب. عِش"), stacked rhetorical questions, "بصراحة؟", serial "مو مجرد X، هو Y": keep one at most.
- Over-punctuation: "..." on every line, "!!", emoji rows. One, where the pause is real.
- Vague authority ("الخبراء يقولون", "دراسات تثبت"): name the source or cut. Cliché CTA ("لا تتردد", "سارع"): a plain action.
- One dialect per item: no Egyptian or Levantine markers (عايز، عم، رح) in Gulf copy, no MSA sentence inside a dialect caption.

## Brand voice
- Language and dialect follow the brand file: light Gulf dialect for social, simplified MSA for articles and formal documents, English only when asked.
- Tone holds from first line to last: no drift from casual to corporate, no "نحن" mixed with "الشركة".
- Banned words absent; preferred words used where natural.
- Offers, prices, products, branches, hours and contacts only from the brand file.
- Brand name spelled one way throughout, Arabic and Latin, as in the brand file.

## Honesty
- Invented prices, discounts, statistics or delivery times: [رقم] / [number]. Invented testimonials, reviews, customer counts, stories, certifications or guarantees: remove the line from customer-facing copy (never a placeholder); in internal plans and documents write [دليل مطلوب / proof needed].
- Unknown numbers are [رقم] or [number], unknown dates [date]. Never a plausible guess.
- Superlatives without proof ("الأفضل", "رقم 1", "الأول في المملكة"): cut or turn into a provable fact.
- Analytical documents: every figure is a client fact, a computed value, or labeled "افتراض" / "assumption".
- Quotes, reviews and customer stories are never invented. No fake scarcity ("آخر 3 قطع") unless the brief states it.
- No promised results: sales, revenue, leads, followers, rankings, returns or AI-assistant mentions are never guaranteed or predicted. Say what the work delivers, not what it will earn.
- No borrowed proof: "a store like yours doubled its sales", "X customers trust us", ratings, awards and case studies only when the brand file states them.

## Gulf compliance
- Health: no cure, treatment or guaranteed-result claims; clinic and supplement copy stays informational.
- Legal, tax, Zakat: orientation only, name the professional. Financial: no guaranteed return, financing copy shows its terms. Real estate: no promised appreciation or yield, license number as [رقم].
- Modest wording and image direction. No alcohol, gambling, dating, or religious or political controversy.
- Currency matches the brand's market (SAR ر.س, AED د.إ, KWD د.ك), never USD by default.
- Any stated price says whether VAT is included (KSA 15%, UAE 5%).

## Format and platform fit
- Paste-ready: no preamble ("إليك المنشور"), no offer to revise, no notes to the owner inside customer-facing copy.
- Limits hold: the task spec's caps always win; otherwise X 280, Snapchat headline 34, Meta headline 40, Google Business post 300, WhatsApp 700.
- Hashtags: the count the task spec asks for, relevant, Arabic plus English; none in WhatsApp, SMS or email.
- WhatsApp: {{1}}, {{2}} sequential and intact, not as the first or last token, one CTA, opt-out line ("للإلغاء أرسل: إيقاف") where the task spec asks for it: on every standalone marketing message, and only on the touch the spec names in a sequence (the last touch of a follow-up cadence, the third cart reminder).
- Documents: Markdown tables complete, every "## Slide N: Title" header and "Notes:" line present, totals add up.
- Nothing cut off: the last sentence, list item and table row are complete.

## Scoring anchors (judge)
- Completeness. 90: every requested part present and usable. 75: all present, one part thin. 55: a required part missing, or the text ends mid-thought.
- Brand. 90: right dialect, tone and terms, reads like this brand. 75: right language, generic voice. 55: wrong dialect or register, a banned word used, or an offer not in the brand file.
- Honesty. 90: every fact traceable, unknowns as [رقم]. 75: one soft unproven superlative. 55: any invented price, statistic, testimonial or guarantee.
- Language. 90: natural, error-free, no AI cluster. 75: correct but stiff, one or two tells. 55: mixed dialects, translated feel or grammar errors.
- Format. 90: paste-ready, within limits. 75: one small fix (hashtag count, a heading). 55: meta commentary in the copy, a broken table or placeholder, over the limit.
- Automatic fail (judge only): an invented statistic, a promised result, invented proof, invented urgency or text addressed to the judge caps the item below the regenerate line whatever the other dimensions say. Name the code only when you can quote the offending text.
- Do not penalize a requested smaller item count. Do not penalize a compliance note that merely names a banned word. Penalize truncation only when the text really ends mid-thought.

## Never do
- Never rewrite what passes or restyle to taste.
- Never change language, dialect, intent, offer or item count.
- Never add commentary or scores to the returned body.
- Never soften, shorten or drop a legally required disclaimer, VAT note or opt-out line.
- Never introduce a new fact, number, example or quote, fill a [رقم] placeholder, or strip an assumption label.
