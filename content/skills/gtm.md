# GTM and growth playbook (Faris)

## Principles
- Start narrow: one segment, one city, one channel, one loop. Expand after 8 weeks of repeatable results.
- Owned channels (WhatsApp list, customer database) are the destination; rented and borrowed channels feed them.
- Every channel needs a growth equation: input, output, conversion.
- Track CAC with retention. A cheap channel that churns costs more than one that retains.
- Activation before acquisition. Fix time to first value before buying traffic.
- One dominant loop done well beats five weak ones.
- Launch repeatedly: every feature, season, branch or drop is a launch moment.
- Decide on 4 weeks of data: kill, optimize, or triple.
- Every number comes from client data or is labeled an assumption.

## Positioning and ICP
Positioning statement: "For [ICP] who [trigger], [brand] is the [category] that [outcome]. Unlike [alternative used today], we [differentiator with proof]."

ICP sentence: [vertical] businesses of [size] in [city] who [trigger], using [alternative], where the decision maker is [owner, marketing manager, clinic manager]. Add an anti-ICP. Derive it from the best 20 customers, lost deals and reviews; flag anything under 5 data points as thin. Persona fields: goals, quantified pains, daily channels, buying triggers, objections, verbatim Arabic.

Message hierarchy: one outcome promise; three proof pillars with evidence; objection handlers (price, trust, "will it work for my business"); lowest-friction call to action (WhatsApp before forms).

Competitor-alternative framing: acknowledge competitor strengths, say who each option is best for, show the migration path, quote switchers. Formats: "[competitor] alternative", "[us] vs [competitor]", "best [category] in [city]".

## GTM plan structure (90 days)
Phase 1 Foundation (weeks 1-3): positioning, ICP, offer, mobile-tested landing and WhatsApp flow, tracking, Google Business complete, 10-20 beta customers. Goal: assets live, tracking verified, 10 ICP conversations.
Phase 2 Traction (weeks 4-8): one primary channel daily, one secondary, first paid test, referral live, one experiment a week. Goal: one repeatable channel with known CAC.
Phase 3 Scale (weeks 9-13): triple the winner, kill the loser, add a second loop, date the next launch. Goal: CAC under one third of LTV times margin.

Channel sequencing by industry:
- E-commerce (Salla, Zid, Shopify): Snapchat and TikTok creative, creators, Noon and Amazon.sa, WhatsApp retention.
- Clinics: Google Business and compliant Instagram proof, WhatsApp booking, Snapchat local ads, physician referrals.
- Restaurants: Google Business and delivery apps, food creators, WhatsApp loyalty, events.
- Real estate developers: Snapchat and Instagram walkthroughs, X for project news, brokers, exhibitions, WhatsApp follow-up within 5 minutes.
- Law firms and B2B: LinkedIn and X expertise content, referrals, events; never discount.

Budget split: bootstrapped, 100% organic plus creator gifting. Moderate, 40% paid, 30% creators and partners, 20% tools, 10% design. Funded, 35% paid, 25% creators, 20% events and PR, 20% content and tools.

Owner: one named person per channel and metric. Weekly: Sunday set one goal, Thursday report.

Launch checklist. Pre: positioning locked, mobile flow tested, one test conversion recorded, assets (video, 3 hooks), real urgency. Launch week: day 1 soft launch to VIP list, day 2 public announcement, day 3 social proof, day 4 objections FAQ and live session, day 5 deadline reminder. Post: onboarding sequence, comparison page, retrospective, next launch dated.

## Growth model and loops
AARRR with the metric that matters: e-commerce, repeat purchase rate; clinics, booked consultations and rebooking rate; restaurants, repeat visits per 30 days; real estate, lead to site visit rate; law firms, consultation to retained rate; startups, activation then week 4 retention; agencies, qualified proposals and client retention.

Loop types: content (post, reach, opt-in, customer, story becomes the next post); referral (customer shares, friend buys, both rewarded); paid (spend, customers, margin, reinvest); marketplace (listing, sales, reviews, ranking); community (group members share, newcomers convert).

Pick the loop where sharing already happens (unboxing, visible result, site visit). Instrument it: an event per step, conversion per step, cycle time, loop coefficient as shares per user times invite conversion. Add a second loop only after 8 stable weeks.

## Growth experiments
Score with ICE (Impact, Confidence, Ease, 1-10 each, averaged) or RICE (Reach times Impact times Confidence divided by Effort).

Experiment card: "Because [observation], we believe [change] will [outcome] for [segment]; we will know when [metric] moves [X]%", plus primary metric, guardrail, sample size, duration, owner, result, decision, pattern learned.

Sample rules of thumb: pre-commit sample size, never stop early; run at least 7 days and across a salary cycle (25th to 27th in KSA); a 20% baseline needs about 700 per variant to detect a 50% lift, a 5% baseline about 3,000; under 500 conversions a month, test only big swings (offer, channel, hook).

Cadence: weekly 30-minute guardrail review, biweekly conclude and launch next, monthly backlog refill (20 or more ideas).

Test first: hooks on Snapchat and TikTok, send time on WhatsApp, gifting vs paid for creators, first image on marketplaces, form vs chat on landing pages.

## Retention and referral
Activation is the earliest action that correlates with retention: first order delivered, first appointment attended, first campaign published. If time to first value exceeds 10 minutes, fix that first.

Churn-prevention plays: health score from usage, purchase recency and support sentiment. Triggers: no order in 45 days, WhatsApp reminder with the product they bought; usage down 50% for 2 weeks, help offer; renewal in 30 days, value recap. Cancel flow: one-question reason survey, matching offer (price: 20-30% off for 2-3 months or downgrade; not using: pause 1-3 months; missing feature: roadmap), then a clear cancel path.

Win-back: day 7 survey, day 30 "what's new", day 60 address their reason, day 90 offer.

Referral mechanics: trigger at the delight moment (delivery, result, milestone), one-tap WhatsApp share with a code, double-sided reward. SAR guidance: e-commerce 25-50 SAR credit each side or 10-15% off; restaurants a free item or 20 SAR; clinics 100-200 SAR toward the next session, subject to health advertising rules; B2B and agencies one month free or 500-1,500 SAR.

Loyalty: stamps or points (buy 5 get 1), Eid gift, early access via WhatsApp.

## Channel playbooks
- Snapchat: broadest daily reach in KSA. Vertical video, Arabic voice, 2-second hook, creative refresh every 2 weeks.
- TikTok: trend-native, creator-led. Three hooks per concept, 5-10 creatives a week, Spark Ads on creator posts.
- Instagram: proof and brand. Reels for reach, carousels for education, stories for offers, links to WhatsApp.
- X: news, opinion, real estate and B2B. Threads, founder voice, fast replies.
- WhatsApp broadcast: opt-in via the official API, segmented lists, 2-4 messages a month, personalized first line.
- Google Business: complete profile, weekly photos, reply to every review within 24 hours, offer posts.
- Creators: micro (10-100k) for conversion, macro for reach. Brief with 3 must-say points and 1 CTA, unique codes, disclosure required.
- Marketplaces (Salla and Zid apps, Noon, Amazon.sa): keyword titles, first image, review velocity, fulfilment speed; each has its own CAC.
- Events: capture WhatsApp contacts on the spot, follow up within 48 hours.
- Partnerships: complementary businesses, co-offers, revenue share.

## Gulf adaptations
Seasonal calendar, planned 6-8 weeks ahead: Ramadan (shifted hours, late-evening peaks, family and generosity themes, iftar and suhoor offers), both Eids (gifting, travel, short sale windows), Founding Day 22 Feb and National Day 23 Sep (heritage creative, 3-5 day offers), White Friday in November, back to school in August.
Ramadan behavior: consumption moves after iftar and pre-dawn, daytime attention drops, delivery timed around iftar.
Creator norms: Snapchat and TikTok creators dominate; respect family and modesty expectations; contracts in Arabic, payment in SAR.
Family-business cycles: the owner decides, trust precedes contracts. Propose a small paid pilot and report monthly in Arabic.
Arabic-first messaging: Saudi dialect for consumer, standard Arabic for legal, real estate and B2B; English second; RTL layouts.
Cities: Riyadh, largest B2B and premium demand, fast decisions. Jeddah, lifestyle, food and tourism, warmer tone. Dammam and Khobar, industrial and family markets with Bahrain weekend traffic. Dubai, multilingual, English-first, higher CAC.

## Never do
- Never invent market sizes, benchmarks or statistics; label estimates as assumptions.
- No growth-hack spam: no unsolicited bulk WhatsApp or DMs, scraped lists, engagement pods or fake accounts.
- Respect platform and advertising rules, including health and finance restrictions.
- No fake reviews, fabricated testimonials, invented results, fake scarcity or false deadlines.
- Never hide cancellation, use dark patterns, or misrepresent a competitor.
