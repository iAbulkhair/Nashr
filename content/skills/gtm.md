# GTM and growth playbook (Faris)

## Principles
- Start narrow: one segment, city, channel and one dominant loop; expand after 8 weeks of repeatable results.
- Owned channels (WhatsApp list, customer database) are the destination; rented and borrowed ones feed them.
- Every channel has a growth equation: input, output, conversion. Judge CAC with retention: a cheap channel that churns costs more.
- Activation before acquisition, offer before traffic: fix time to first value and the weakest offer lever first.
- Every feature, season, branch or drop is a launch. Decide on 4 weeks of data: stop, fix, or scale in steps.
- Evidence: every number comes from the owner's data, the workspace data provided, or a computation from them (show the formula); anything else is [رقم] / [number] or labelled افتراض / assumption. Keep facts, assumptions and projections on separate lines.
- Goals are outputs (assets live, experiments run, plays launched). Results (sales, leads, followers, rankings, ROI) are KPIs with a target [رقم] / [number] (owner to set), never promises.
- Proof rule: reviews, counts, customer stories and before-after only from the brand file or owner. Missing proof in internal plans: [دليل مطلوب / proof needed]; customer-facing copy leaves an unsourced proof line out, never a placeholder.
- Quiet hours: Business WhatsApp and SMS go out between 9:00 and 22:00 local time, never at prayer times or Friday midday; in Ramadan shift later (after iftar and taraweeh).

## Positioning and ICP
Positioning statement: "For [ICP] who [trigger], [brand] is the [category] that [outcome]. Unlike [alternative used today], we [differentiator with proof]."

ICP: [vertical] businesses of [size] in [city] who [trigger], using [alternative], decided by [role]; plus an anti-ICP. From the best 20 customers, lost deals and reviews; under 5 data points is thin. Persona: goals, pains, channels, triggers, objections, verbatim Arabic.

Message hierarchy: one core outcome in the buyer's words; up to three proof pillars under the proof rule; objection handlers (price, trust, "will it work for my business"); lowest-friction CTA (WhatsApp before forms).

Offer value = (desired outcome × believed likelihood) ÷ (time to first result × effort); score each lever 1-5 as the buyer sees it, fix the weakest before price. Likelihood: real proof only (owner's reviews, consented before-after except clinics and health, demos, a guarantee); time: done-for-you parts; effort: WhatsApp ordering, mada, Apple Pay, Tabby or Tamara, delivery. Offers, guarantees and prices are proposals until owner-approved.

Versus competitors: fair strengths, who each option suits, migration path, real switchers only.

## GTM plan structure (90 days)
Phase 1 Foundation (weeks 1-3): positioning, ICP, offer, mobile-tested landing and WhatsApp flow, tracking check, Google Business, AI-assistant prompt list, [رقم] beta customers from warm contacts. Goal: assets live, tracking verified, [رقم] ICP conversations.
Phase 2 Traction (weeks 4-8): one primary channel daily, one secondary, referral live, first paid test if the paid gate allows, one experiment a week. Goal: one channel with measured CAC.
Phase 3 Scale (weeks 9-13): scale the winner in budget steps, stop the loser, add a second loop, date the next launch. Goal: each scale decision logged against the paid gate.
Paid gate: paid tests start only once the business has revenue and a working conversion path (before that: organic, creators, WhatsApp); scaling paid needs CAC payback within the target AND retention flattening across at least [رقم] / [number] cohorts of the owner's own data (the owner sets it; three is a heuristic). Payback = CAC ÷ (ARPA × GM%) months.

Lead sources in order: warm contacts and referrals validate the offer; one engine (content, creators or personal B2B outreach); paid once the paid gate allows; then staff, partners, affiliates. No agency or affiliate on an unproven channel.

Channel sequencing by industry:
- E-commerce (Salla, Zid, Shopify): Snapchat and TikTok creative, creators, Noon and Amazon.sa, WhatsApp retention.
- Clinics: Google Business, physician referrals, informational Instagram and real reviews (no before-after or outcome claims), WhatsApp booking, Snapchat local ads.
- Restaurants: Google Business and delivery apps, food creators, WhatsApp loyalty, events.
- Real estate: Snapchat and Instagram walkthroughs, X for project news, brokers, exhibitions, WhatsApp follow-up within 5 minutes.
- Law firms and B2B: referrals, LinkedIn and X expertise content, events; no promotional discounts.

Budget split (heuristic, افتراض / assumption until owner data and measured CAC replace it; paid only past the paid gate): bootstrapped, organic plus creator gifting; moderate, 40% paid, 30% creators and partners, 20% tools, 10% design; funded, 35% paid, 25% creators, 20% events and PR, 20% content and tools.

Owner: one named person per channel and metric; goal on the first workday (Sunday KSA, Monday UAE), report on the last.

Launch checklist. Pre: positioning locked, mobile flow and test conversion done, video and 3 hooks. Days 1-5: VIP soft launch, public, real social proof (else skip the day), objections FAQ and live session, an owner-set deadline reminder only. Post: onboarding, comparison page, retrospective, next launch dated.

## Growth model and loops
Key AARRR metric: e-commerce, repeat purchase rate; clinics, booked consultations and rebooking; restaurants, 30-day repeat-visit rate and 90-day retention rate; real estate, lead to site visit; law firms, consultation to retained; agencies, qualified proposals and client retention.

Loops: content (post, reach, opt-in, customer, story); referral (share, friend buys, both rewarded); paid (spend, customers, margin, reinvest); marketplace (listing, sales, reviews, ranking). Pick the loop where sharing already happens (unboxing, visible result). Instrument each step (event, conversion, cycle time); loop coefficient = shares per user × invite conversion.

## Growth experiments
ICE: Impact, Confidence, Ease, 1-10 each, averaged (Confidence 8-10 own data, 5-7 comparable evidence, 1-4 hunch); order cards by it.

Hypothesis: "Because [observation], we believe [change] for [segment] will move [metric] by at least [رقم]": the smallest change that pays for the test, else [رقم] (owner to set).

Locked before launch: primary metric, one guardrail (margin, refunds, complaints, opt-outs), threshold, sample size, duration, rollback (trigger, who, by when).
Sample per variant ≈ 16 × p(1−p) ÷ d² (p = baseline rate from owner data, d = absolute change to detect; about 80% power, 5% two-sided). Duration = total sample ÷ daily eligible visitors, whole weeks spanning a payday window (KSA government salaries land around the 27th; private payroll varies). Too long: a bigger swing, a holdout city or branch, or a labelled directional pilot.
Stop early only when a guardrail breaks (then roll back), never on a good-looking result. k comparisons: significance 0.05 ÷ k. Randomize by customer; one change per test; no overlapping tests on one audience. Report the change with its range; a range crossing zero is inconclusive.

Cadence: weekly guardrail review; biweekly, conclude tests at the locked sample, launch the next; monthly, refill the backlog (20+ ideas). First tests: hooks, WhatsApp send time (quiet hours apply), creator gifting vs paid, marketplace first image, form vs chat.

## Retention and referral
Activation: the earliest action that predicts retention. Default (store: first delivered order plus second visit; clinic: first completed booking) is افتراض / assumption until a retained-vs-churned cohort check on owner data confirms it.

Onboarding: welcome with one next step; nudge if not activated by day [رقم]; help offer; at the first result, ask for a review or referral.

Churn prevention: health score (adoption, outcomes, relationship, support, commercial). Triggers (owner's cycle): no order in 1.5 × their reorder interval, reminder with their product; usage down [رقم]% for 2 weeks, help offer; term renewal 90 days out, value recap. Cancel flow: one-question reason, matching save offer (price: downgrade or [رقم]% off for [رقم] months; not using: pause [رقم] months; missing feature: a committed item or a workaround, never a promised date), then a clear cancel path. Every save and win-back offer is within the discount policy: owner-approved and time-boxed.

Win-back: day 0 confirmation, day 7 survey, day 30 "what's new", day 60 address their reason if fixed, day 90 final offer.

Referral: ask at the delight moment (delivery, result, milestone); one-tap WhatsApp share with a code; double-sided reward: store credit or % off (e-commerce), free item (restaurants), next-session credit (clinics, within health advertising rules), free month (B2B). Reward per side ≤ first-order gross margin × [رقم] (owner-set share), within the discount policy; show the math, else [رقم] [currency]; state whether VAT (15% KSA, 5% UAE) is included.

Loyalty: stamps or points (buy [رقم] get 1), Eid gift, early access via WhatsApp.

## Channel playbooks
- Snapchat: often strong KSA reach (assumption; check owner data); vertical video, Arabic voice, 2-second hook, refresh every 2 weeks or on fatigue.
- TikTok: trend-native, creator-led; 3 hooks per concept, 5-10 creatives a week, Spark Ads on creator posts.
- Instagram: brand and real proof only; reels for reach, carousels to educate, stories for offers, links to WhatsApp.
- WhatsApp broadcast: official API opt-in, segmented lists, 2-4 messages a month, personalized first line, quiet hours.
- Google Business: complete profile, weekly photos, reply to every review within 24 hours, offer posts.
- Creators: micro (10-100k followers) for trust and conversion tests, macro for reach; brief: 3 must-say points, 1 CTA, unique code, disclosure.
- Marketplaces (Salla and Zid apps, Noon, Amazon.sa): keyword titles, first image, review velocity, fulfilment speed; separate CAC each.
- Events: capture WhatsApp opt-ins on the spot, follow up within 48 hours. Partners: co-offers, revenue share.

## Lead magnets and content repurposing
One magnet per persona and stage, for one narrow buyer problem now: awareness (checklist, short videos), consideration (price guide, comparison, calculator), decision (template, account audit, short free consultation). It shows the problem's cost (owner data or [رقم]) or samples the product, answers the top pre-purchase question, works on a phone in 10 minutes; its title names deliverable and problem, never a business outcome.

Delivery: click-to-WhatsApp with a prefilled keyword ("أرسل كلمة دليل"), not a form; name plus one qualifying question; a clear yes to stated content and frequency before any broadcast list; sent within a minute with a stop word and one next step. Gate price guides, calculators and templates, not reach content. Track opt-in to qualified chat and to sale, days to sale and opt-outs per magnet and source; kill a magnet that fills the list, not the pipeline, in 4 weeks.

Repurposing: one core asset a month (guide, real consented customer story, Q&A) split into 5-8 points: Snapchat/TikTok, one point per vertical video, dialect hook; Instagram, carousel and reel; X/LinkedIn, standard Arabic or English; WhatsApp/email, 3-line summary. Change hook, length, dialect and CTA, never the promise, facts or numbers; one keyword or code per channel.

## Paid acquisition readiness
Tracking check before scaling paid (pass or fail each):
- Pixel per platform (Meta, Snap, TikTok, Google) on key pages plus server-side conversions API, deduplicated by event ID.
- One primary conversion per campaign (purchase, attended booking, qualified WhatsApp chat) valued in the brand's currency.
- One lowercase UTM scheme (source, medium, campaign as [season]_[offer], content as [hook]); a WhatsApp keyword or code per campaign.
- One source of truth (store, booking system, or a sheet of WhatsApp and phone orders by source); check platform counts against it, never sum them.
- A test conversion appears in the platform and in the source of truth; consent per KSA or UAE PDPL.
Structure: search by brand, non-brand, competitor, language, with negatives (وظائف, مجاني, jobs); paid social: prospecting (customers excluded), retargeting, customers. Test overlapping channels' incrementality (holdout city or paused period) before moving budget.

## Gulf adaptations
Seasonal calendar; preparation starts 4 to 6 weeks ahead (6 to 8 for a full launch): Ramadan (attention after iftar and pre-dawn, delivery around iftar, generosity themes, iftar and suhoor offers), both Eids (gifting, travel, short sale windows), Founding Day 22 Feb and Saudi National Day 23 Sep (heritage creative, 3-5 day offers), UAE National Day 2 Dec, Kuwait National and Liberation Days 25-26 Feb, White Friday in November, back to school in August.
Creator norms: respect family and modesty expectations; contracts in Arabic, local-currency payment; in KSA confirm the creator's Mawthooq (موثوق) licence.
Family business: the owner decides, trust precedes contracts; propose a small pilot, report monthly in Arabic.
Arabic first: Saudi dialect for consumer, standard Arabic for legal, real estate and B2B; English second; RTL; amounts in the brand's currency (SAR default; AED, KWD).
Cities (heuristics; check owner sales by city): Riyadh, B2B and premium; Jeddah, lifestyle, food, tourism; Khobar, Bahrain weekend traffic; Dubai, English-first, crowded auctions.

## Never do
- Never invent market sizes, benchmarks, statistics, reviews, testimonials or results, or cite another business's results as proof; no fake scarcity or false deadlines.
- Never promise sales, leads, followers, rankings, AI citations or ROI.
- No growth-hack spam: unsolicited bulk WhatsApp or DMs, scraped lists, engagement pods, fake accounts.
- Never start or scale paid outside the paid gate, or scale before the tracking check passes.
- Respect platform and advertising rules, including health and finance restrictions.
- Never hide cancellation, use dark patterns, or misrepresent a competitor.
