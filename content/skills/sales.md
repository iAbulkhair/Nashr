# Sales enablement playbook (Sami)

## Principles
- Qualify early and ruthlessly; an hour on the wrong lead is a zero.
- Discovery before demo. No product tour or price list before you know the pain.
- Sell the gap in the buyer's own numbers (orders/month, bookings/week, SAR per lead), never features.
- WhatsApp-first, relationship-first. Reply to inbound within 5 minutes in working hours.
- One ask per message, low friction. "Does this sound relevant?" beats "Book a 30-minute call".
- Multi-thread: in family businesses the person on WhatsApp rarely signs.
- Urgency only from real events: Ramadan, store launch, White Friday, contract expiry.
- No conversation ends without a dated next step; be transparent on price and VAT.

## Lead qualification
Score 0-100 across five dimensions. Score only what you have evidence for; mark unknowns and ask.

| Dimension | Max | Full | Partial | Low | Zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fit | 25 | Target vertical (store, clinic, restaurant, real estate, law, startup, agency), GCC, active online | Adjacent vertical (15) | Weak (8) | Outside ICP (0) |
| Intent | 20 | Asked pricing or demo, named a use case | Inquiry with a stated pain (12) | Browsing (5) | None (0) |
| Budget | 20 | Stated budget or paid spend in range | Owner can create budget if ROI is clear (12) | "Need to find budget" (5) | Cannot afford entry tier (0) |
| Timing | 20 | Compelling event within 30 days | Within 90 days (12) | "This year" (5) | None (0) |
| Authority | 15 | Owner, GM or marketing lead who signs | Influencer with access to signer (9) | Staff, no access (4) | Wrong contact (0) |

Bands: 80-100 Hot, reply in 5 minutes, call today, senior rep. 60-79 Warm, same-day reply, discovery within 48 hours. 40-59 Cool, two qualifying questions, weekly nurture. Below 40, monthly nurture or graceful exit. Disqualify: no GCC operations, cannot afford entry tier, no path to the signer, competitor contract in force.

## Discovery and call scripts
WhatsApp opening: salam, name and title, how they reached us, one line of reason, one question. Under 40 words, no brochure, no price. Ask before calling: "Is a 10-minute call today or tomorrow better, or do you prefer here?" Phone: 10 seconds of context, then permission to ask questions and a promise to say so if it's not a fit.

Five-step call (20-30 minutes):
1. Open (3 min): rapport, agenda, permission.
2. Situation (5): channels, platform, tools, team, monthly volume.
3. Pain and impact (10): biggest challenge, what happens when it breaks, cost in SAR or hours.
4. Decision and money (5): who else, how they decide, budget range, timeline, other options.
5. Close (3): restate 2-3 pains in their words, propose a dated next step, WhatsApp recap within 2 hours.

Questions by stage:
- First conversation: what prompted this now, what do you use today, when must it be live, who else is involved.
- Discovery: quantify pain (orders, leads, bookings, cost per lead), map stakeholders, evaluation criteria, past attempts and why they failed.
- Pre-proposal: signer engaged, budget and timeline confirmed, competitors known, "what would make you not move forward?".

## Objection handling
Pattern: acknowledge, one clarifying question, reframe to their numbers, show proof, confirm, propose the next step.

| Objection | Root cause | Response pattern | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Value unclear, or negotiating | "Compared to what? What does the problem cost you monthly?" Walk the math, offer a smaller scope | SAR ROI sheet, same-vertical case |
| "Send me info" | Polite exit, or must forward to the owner | "Happy to. Results or price, what matters most? Who else will read it?" Book the follow-up | One-pager |
| "We use someone" | Switching cost, unvoiced frustration | "How is reporting, ROAS, response time? If one thing could be better, what?" Offer an audit | Audit finding, switch story |
| "Not now / after Ramadan" | Competing priorities | "Each month of [pain] costs about X. Can we lock a start date so the season isn't missed?" | Cost of delay, seasonal timeline |
| "Let me think / ask my partner" | Not the signer, or doubt | "What will they ask? I can join a 15-minute call or prepare an Arabic summary" | Owner-ready summary |

## Follow-up cadences
Seven touches over 21 days; each stands alone and adds one new thing.

| Touch | Day | Channel | Intent |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | 0 | WhatsApp | Recap and agreed next step |
| 2 | 2 | WhatsApp | One proof asset from their vertical |
| 3 | 5 | Email | Forwardable summary for the owner or partner |
| 4 | 8 | WhatsApp | New angle: season insight or a finding from their store |
| 5 | 12 | Phone, WhatsApp if missed | Answer questions, propose a date |
| 6 | 16 | Email | Low-friction offer: pilot or seasonal package |
| 7 | 21 | WhatsApp | Breakup: "Reply 1 interested, 2 check back in 3 months, 3 not interested" |

Send Sunday to Thursday, 10:00-13:00 or 16:00-21:00, never in prayer windows or Friday before 16:00. Stop after the breakup; re-engage only on a new trigger after 30-60 days, and stop instantly on any opt-out.

## Proposals and closing
Proposal (5-7 pages, Arabic or bilingual): 1. Summary: their challenge in their words, expected outcome, launch date. 2. Scope mapped to their 2-3 pains. 3. Plan: milestones, owners, launch tied to their season. 4. Investment: 2-3 tiers in SAR, VAT 15% separate, payment terms, inclusions. 5. Proof: one relevant case. 6. Next steps: decision date, signer, kickoff.

Anchoring: show the top tier first, recommend the middle; price against the cost of the problem; every discount is a trade (longer term, upfront payment, referral).

Closing: summary close ("shall we start with the middle plan?"); calendar close anchored to their event ("to be live before Ramadan we sign by the 10th"); assumptive next step ("I'll send the contract tonight, who signs?"); final sweep ("anything that would stop us starting by then?"), then silence. Urgency without pressure: real deadlines, real capacity, honest price validity.

## Pipeline hygiene and forecasting
Stages, exit criteria, probability: New (first reply) 5%; Qualified (score 60+, pain and authority confirmed) 15%; Discovery done (pain quantified, budget, timeline, signer known) 30%; Proposal sent (walkthrough scheduled) 50%; Negotiation (terms, close date agreed) 75%; Verbal yes 90%.

Weighted forecast = sum of value x probability. Report commit (90%+), best case (70-89%), pipeline (50-69%), upside. Expected scenario = 85% commit + 65% best case + 45% pipeline + 10% upside; recalibrate monthly against actual close rates.

Momentum: replies within hours, a second stakeholder joins. Red flags: no reply 7+ days, close date slipped twice, single contact, proposal dark 14+ days, price is the only topic, any stage over 21 days. Hygiene: weekly review, dated next step on every deal, disqualify dead deals, loss reason required.

## Written outreach: email, SMS and WhatsApp text
Email, cold or warm:
- Subject: 2-5 plain words that look internal ("سؤال عن فرع جدة"). Arabic for Arabic readers, one script only. Distrust triggers: fake "Re:" or "Fwd:", caps, "مجاني", "عاجل", exclamation marks, a pitch or deadline.
- First line: salam and title ("السلام عليكم أستاذ خالد"), then an observation about their business that leads to the problem. Never open with who you are. Warm email opens with the shared context: call, referral or inquiry.
- Body: 50-90 words, plain text, no attachments, one link at most. Observation, problem, one proof, one ask.
- Ask: an interest question ("هل أرسل لكم التفاصيل؟"), never a 30-minute call in a first touch.
- Bumps: 2-3 short replies in the same thread (day 3, 7, 14), each with a new angle, proof or resource. Never "just checking in". The last closes politely.

SMS and short WhatsApp text:
- SMS: one segment. Arabic cuts a segment to 70 characters (Latin 160), so use it for reminders and confirmations, not pitches. Registered sender name, business named first, one full branded link at most, a stop instruction in every promotional text.
- WhatsApp: under 300 characters, one idea, one question, no link in a first message, never a pasted email.

Personalization: real is a checkable detail (new branch, menu change, recent review) that explains why you write now. Test: delete it; if the message still works it was decoration. Fake is a first-name token, "أعجبني حسابكم", or praise with no consequence.

Gulf notes: text only inbound, opted-in or referred contacts, and say where the number came from. Send windows follow the cadence rules above. In Ramadan send email late morning and texts after taraweeh, never near iftar. Proof is a client named with permission or the prospect's own numbers.

## Gulf adaptations
- WhatsApp: name and title, short messages, one question at a time, ask before long files or voice notes, nothing before 9:00 or after 22:00. Weekend is Friday and Saturday.
- Prayer and Ramadan: no calls within 15 minutes of prayer. Ramadan hours shift; pitch late morning or after 21:30. Eids close offices; plan campaigns 4-6 weeks ahead.
- Seasons as compelling events: Ramadan, Eids, back to school, National Day 23 Sep, Founding Day 22 Feb, White Friday.
- Decision makers: the owner or a relative signs even when a marketing manager leads. Ask "who signs?" early, offer to present to the owner, honour titles (Abu Fulan, Ustadh, Doctor, Engineer).
- Women-only segments (salons, ladies' gyms, women's clinics, fashion): text over calls, Arabic, no unsolicited calls, female rep where possible, modest creative.
- Money: SAR, VAT 15% shown separately, ZATCA e-invoice. Mada and Apple Pay default at checkout; mention Tabby and Tamara when selling store growth.
- Platforms: Salla and Zid dominate Saudi e-commerce, Shopify for cross-border, Foodics for restaurants. Snapchat, TikTok and Instagram are the main ad channels.
- Arabic vs English: Arabic for local SMBs, English for startups and agencies, bilingual for mixed teams. Polite dialect, not formal MSA. Never machine-translate contract terms.

## Never do
- Never invent numbers, results, client names or benchmarks; use the client's numbers or say "no data yet".
- No fake scarcity or deadlines; "only 2 spots" only if literally true.
- No religious or national sentiment used as pressure.
- Honour opt-out instantly and permanently.
- No messages outside 9:00-22:00, during prayer windows, or Friday before 16:00.
- Never bluff on capabilities; say "let me check" and answer within 24 hours.
- Never trash competitors or the incumbent agency; never quote a price without VAT clarity.
- Never guarantee sales, rankings or results; never discount to rescue a stalled deal; never skip discovery.
