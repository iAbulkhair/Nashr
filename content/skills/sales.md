# Sales enablement playbook (Sami)

## Principles
- Qualify early and ruthlessly.
- Discovery before demo: no product tour or price list before you know the pain.
- Sell the gap in the buyer's own numbers (orders/month, bookings/week, cost per lead), never features.
- Evidence rule: numbers come only from the owner's data, the workspace data provided, or a computation from them with inputs shown. Anything else, unset targets included, is [رقم] / [number] or labelled افتراض / assumption. Keep facts (what the lead said or did) apart from projections.
- Every claim needs brand-file evidence; no empty adjectives ("مميز", "الأفضل"). Proof is only a client, case or result the brand file names, or the prospect's own numbers. With neither, customer-facing copy omits the proof line (never "a business like yours"); internal sheets and plans write [دليل مطلوب / proof needed].
- Set output goals (messages drafted, calls held, next steps dated), never promised results (sales, leads, reply or close rates).
- No reason to contact now means do not send: name why this person, this business, this week.
- WhatsApp-first, relationship-first. Internal target, never promised to the lead: reply to inbound within 5 minutes in working hours.
- One low-friction ask per message. "Does this sound relevant?" beats "Book a 30-minute call".
- Multi-thread: in family businesses the person on WhatsApp rarely signs.
- Urgency only from real events (Ramadan, store launch, White Friday, contract expiry), dates the lead gave, or deadlines, capacity and price validity stated in the brand file or brief. Every conversation ends with a dated next step.

## Lead qualification
ICP must exclude someone. Fit: 2-4 verticals, size band ([رقم] branches, staff or orders/month), market, tools in use. Trigger and pain: what makes them buy now, what breaks, who feels it, today's workaround. Disqualifiers: no GCC operations, below entry tier, no path to the signer, competitor contract in force, wants guaranteed results before paying, needs what the brand file does not offer.

Buying signals, strongest first; they set Intent and Timing:
- Tier 1 active: asked price or a quote, booked a visit or demo, compared options, replied to an offer; act the same day.
- Tier 2 change: branch or store launch, new manager, hiring, renovation, season within 6 weeks, contract ending.
- Tier 3 engagement: follows, saves, one site visit; nurture, don't pitch.

Score 0-100 across five dimensions. Score only what you have evidence for; mark unknowns and ask.

| Dimension | Max | Full | Partial | Low | Zero |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Fit | 25 | ICP vertical and market, active online | Adjacent vertical (15) | Weak (8) | Outside ICP (0) |
| Intent | 20 | Tier 1 signal, named a use case | Inquiry with a stated pain (12) | Tier 3 only (5) | None (0) |
| Budget | 20 | Stated budget or paid spend in range | Owner can create budget if value is clear (12) | "Need to find budget" (5) | Cannot afford entry tier (0) |
| Timing | 20 | Compelling event within 30 days | Within 90 days (12) | "This year" (5) | None (0) |
| Authority | 15 | Owner, GM or marketing lead who signs | Influencer with access to signer (9) | Staff, no access (4) | Wrong contact (0) |

Bands: 80-100 Hot, reply in 5 minutes, call today, owner or best closer. 60-79 Warm, same-day reply, discovery within 48 hours. 40-59 Cool, two qualifying questions, weekly nurture. Below 40, monthly nurture or graceful exit. Any disqualifier ends it. Qualifying questions: two per dimension, open, in the owner's language, tagged.

## Discovery and call scripts
WhatsApp opening: salam, the lead's title and name, one identity clause at most (معك [الاسم] من [النشاط]), how they reached us and why now, one question; under 40 words, no brochure; if they asked the price, give the brand-file price or range with VAT status, else no price. Ask before calling: "A short call today or tomorrow, or here?" Phone: 10 seconds of context, permission to ask, a promise to say if it's not a fit. Never ask what their site, Instagram or Google Maps answer.

Five-step call (20-30 minutes); the buyer talks most:
1. Open (3 min): rapport, agenda, upfront contract ("by the end: a next step, not a fit, or what we still need").
2. Situation (5): channels, platform, tools, team, monthly volume; two questions at most.
3. Pain and impact (10): biggest challenge, what happens when it breaks, cost in SAR or hours; after a hard question, wait.
4. Decision and money (5): who else, how they decide, budget range, timeline, other options.
5. Close (3): restate 2-3 pains in their words, dated next step, WhatsApp recap within 2 hours.

Question bank (a script's 8 span situation, pain, impact, decision):
- Situation: "How do you handle [process] today?" "What prompted this now?"
- Pain: "Where does it break?" "A recent example?" "Since when?"
- Impact: "What does that cost in orders, hours or SAR?" "What did you try, why didn't it stick?" "If nothing changes before [season]?"
- Gap: today (tools, problem, cost, root cause) against solved (what, by when). If they can close it alone, there is no deal.
- Decision: "Who else weighs in?" "How did you choose last time?" "What would stop this?" "When must it be live?"
- Need-payoff, to close: "If [pain] were handled, what would that free up?" Their words feed the recap and proposal.

Pre-proposal check (yes / no / unknown with evidence; each unknown is the next question): Metric (their number, their words), Money-holder (who decides the spend; spoken to?), Criteria, Process (steps and dates to start), Paperwork (contract, PO, VAT invoice, ZATCA, payment terms), Pain (cost of doing nothing), Champion (test: will they set the signer meeting?), Competition (incumbent, in-house, a relative, doing nothing). No proposal until pain, money-holder and timeline are yes.

## Objection handling
Loop: acknowledge, explore (one question: value, timing, trust, authority or status quo?), respond (their numbers, brand-file proof), confirm ("does that answer it?"), next step. Price objections usually mean doubt about value: rebuild the gap, not the price. Pilots, smaller scopes and packages only as the brand file lists them, else [owner to define]. Rows show the pattern: write the 8 objections this brand's buyers raise, never these rows as-is.

| Objection | Root cause | Response | Proof |
|---|---|---|---|
| "Too expensive" | Value unclear, or haggling | "Compared to what? What does the problem cost monthly?" Walk the math; offer a brand-file smaller step | Their numbers, computed |
| "Send me info" | Polite exit, or for the owner | "Happy to. What matters most, and who else reads it?" Book the follow-up | Brand-file facts |
| "Not now / after Ramadan" | Competing priorities | "What does a month of [pain] cost? Shall we lock a start date before the season?" | Season timeline, cost of delay |
| "Let me ask my partner" | Not the signer, or doubt | "What will they ask? I can join a short call or send an Arabic summary" | Owner-ready summary |
| "Guarantee results?" | Risk, burned before | "We commit to deliverables and dates. What would you need to see by [date]?" Offer a brand-file pilot | Scope, review dates |

## Follow-up cadences
Seven touches: five WhatsApp under 400 characters, one email, one call script. Each adds one new thing, has one CTA and its day, channel and intent in the title; never "just checking in".

| Day | Channel | Intent and new value |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | WhatsApp | Thanks and recap: their request, the quote or visit, one question |
| 1 | WhatsApp | Answer the likely open question (scope, payment, delivery), brand-file facts |
| 3 | Email | Forwardable summary for the owner: quote [رقم], inclusions, VAT, validity [date] |
| 6 | WhatsApp | New angle: season timing, a tip, a real brand-file case |
| 10 | Call (WhatsApp note if missed) | Check fit, surface the real objection, propose a dated step |
| 15 | WhatsApp | Smaller first step from the brand file (pilot, reduced scope, package), else [owner to define]; no new discount |
| 21 | WhatsApp | Breakup: "Reply 1 interested, 2 later (in [رقم] months), 3 not interested", plus the opt-out line |

Quiet hours: business WhatsApp and SMS go out 9:00-22:00 local, never at prayer times or Friday midday; in Ramadan shift later (after iftar and taraweeh). Best slots: working days, 10:00-13:00 or 16:00-21:00; off-slot touches wait. A reply ends the cadence. After the breakup, re-engage only on a new trigger after 30-60 days.

## Proposals and closing
Proposal (5-7 pages, Arabic or bilingual), three parts: 1. Understanding: challenge, deadline, cost of waiting, in their words; if it would read true for another client, rewrite it. 2. Approach: scope mapped to their 2-3 pains, milestones, owners, launch tied to their season. 3. Commitment: deliverables, dates, review points; 2-3 tiers (local currency, VAT separate; prices, terms and inclusions only from the brand file, else [رقم] SAR, [inclusions]); decision date, signer, kickoff.

Win themes (2-3): their need + our capability + brand-file proof, repeated in summary, approach and pricing. No proof, no theme.

Anchoring: before price, cut time to first deliverable and effort asked; show the top tier first, recommend the middle; price against the cost of the problem; every discount is a trade the owner's discount policy allows (annual prepay, volume, dated seasonal window, retention save offer, referral reward) within the owner's cap, approver and end date; a longer term without prepay earns a non-price concession (setup included, extra service), not a discount.

Closing: summary ("shall we start with the middle plan?"); calendar, anchored to their event ("to be live before Ramadan we sign by [date]"); assumptive ("I'll send the contract tonight, who signs?"); final sweep ("anything that would stop us?"), then silence.

## Pipeline hygiene and forecasting
Stages and exit criteria: New (first reply); Qualified (60+, pain and authority confirmed); Discovery done (pain quantified, budget, timeline, signer); Proposal sent (walkthrough booked); Negotiation (terms, close date); Verbal yes. Weights 5/15/30/50/75/90% are a default; prefer the owner's stage-to-close history and say which you used. Forecast = sum of value x weight, as a range with gaps and assumptions stated: Commit = signed, or a verbal yes with a named signer and a date; Best case = qualified and active; Pipeline = earlier stages.

Momentum: fast replies, a second stakeholder joins. A deal counts as stalled after 14 days with no reply and no scheduled next step; cadence touch days are actions, not status thresholds. Red flags: close date slipped twice, single contact, champion won't introduce the signer, price the only topic, a deal over 1.5x the owner's median stage time (21 days when there is no history). Hygiene: weekly review, dated next step on every deal, dead deals closed with a loss reason (qualification, execution, competition).

## Written outreach: email, SMS and WhatsApp text
First message: reason now (a signal or checkable observation), relevance in their words, proof only if real, one low-friction ask. Cold email never opens with who you are. Triggers and observations only from lead data given; in templates write [trigger], [observation].
- Email subject: 2-5 plain internal-looking words ("سؤال عن فرع [المدينة]"), one alphabet (Arabic for Arabic readers). Distrust triggers: fake "Re:" or "Fwd:", caps, "مجاني", "عاجل", exclamation marks, a pitch or deadline.
- First line: salam and title, then at once an observation that leads to the problem. Warm email opens with the shared context.
- Body: 50-90 words, plain text, no attachments, one link at most: observation, problem, one brand-file proof if any (else omit), one interest ask ("هل أرسل لكم التفاصيل؟").
- SMS: one segment; Arabic cuts it to 70 characters (Latin 160), so reminders and confirmations, not pitches. Registered sender, business named first, one branded link at most, a stop instruction in every promotional text.
- WhatsApp: first message under 300 characters (the task cap is the ceiling), one idea, one question, no link, never a pasted email.

Personalization: real is a checkable detail from the lead data (new branch, menu change, recent review) that explains why now; if the message works without it, it was decoration. Fake: a first-name token, "أعجبني حسابكم", empty praise.

Consent: text only inbound, opted-in or referred contacts; say where the number came from.

## Gulf adaptations
- WhatsApp: ask before long files or voice notes. Weekend Fri-Sat, but Sat-Sun in the UAE (Friday half day); send days follow the brand's market.
- Prayer and Ramadan: no calls within 15 minutes of prayer. In Ramadan, call late morning or after taraweeh, never near iftar. Eids close offices; prepare seasons 4-6 weeks ahead (6-8 for a full launch).
- Compelling seasons: Ramadan, Eids, back to school, Saudi National Day 23 Sep, Founding Day 22 Feb, UAE National Day 2 Dec, White Friday.
- Decision makers: the owner or a relative signs even when a marketing manager leads. Ask "who signs?" early, offer to present to the owner, honour titles (Abu Fulan, Ustadh, Doctor, Engineer).
- Women-only segments (salons, ladies' gyms, women's clinics, fashion): text over calls, Arabic, female rep where possible, modest creative.
- Money: the market's currency (SAR by default), VAT shown separately (15% KSA, 5% UAE; other markets: rate from the brand file or [رقم]%), ZATCA e-invoice in KSA. Mention Tabby and Tamara when selling store growth.
- Platforms to ask about in discovery, never claims in copy: Salla, Zid, Shopify, Foodics; ad channels Snapchat, TikTok, Instagram.
- Arabic vs English: Arabic for local SMBs, English for startups and agencies, bilingual for mixed teams. Polite dialect, not formal MSA. Never machine-translate contract terms.

## Never do
- Never invent numbers, results, client names, case studies or benchmarks; use the client's numbers or [رقم] / [number].
- Never guarantee sales, rankings, reply rates or results.
- No fake scarcity or deadlines; "only [رقم] spots" only if stated in the brand file or brief. No religious or national sentiment as pressure.
- Honour opt-out instantly and permanently; no volume blasts to cold lists.
- No business WhatsApp or SMS outside quiet hours: 9:00-22:00 local, never at prayer times or Friday midday, later in Ramadan.
- Never bluff on capabilities; say "let me check" and answer within 24 hours.
- Never trash competitors or the incumbent agency; never quote a price without VAT clarity.
- Never discount to rescue a stalled deal; never skip discovery.
