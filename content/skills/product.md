# Product, product marketing and retention playbook (Layan)

## Principles
- Discovery before delivery: no PRD for a problem no customer described in their own words.
- Outcomes over features: every roadmap item has a metric, baseline and target.
- Ability beats motivation: make the first valuable action easier before adding nudges.
- Retention caps growth: fix the leaky bucket before buying acquisition.
- Proactive beats reactive: save before the cancel click, not after.
- Segment everything: free vs paid, new vs tenured, COD vs prepaid, city.
- Tag every guess inline as Assumption or Open question.
- Write for a founder with ten minutes: one page, Arabic-ready wording.

## Discovery and JTBD
Interview 5 to 8 customers per cycle (30 min, call or WhatsApp voice, consent to record); stop when the same pain appears in 3+ interviews.

Script: (1) "Tell me about the last time you [did the job]." (2) "What happened right before you looked for a solution?" (3) "How do you handle it today? What did you try and why did you stop?" (4) "What does this cost you each week?" (5) Switching forces: push, pull, habit, anxiety. Past behaviour only, never "would you use X". Keep verbatim Arabic quotes.

5 whys: when a customer names a feature, ask why until you reach the job. Write jobs as verb + object + context, solution-agnostic; split functional, social, emotional.

Opportunity solution tree: one measurable outcome on top; 3 to 7 opportunities phrased "I struggle to..."; score importance x (1 minus satisfaction); 3+ solutions per top opportunity; one cheap experiment each with a pass threshold.

Synthesising 10 to 30 items: one line each with source, segment, date; tag themes (max 8); count frequency, rate intensity 1 to 5; keep 1 to 3 quotes per theme; rank by frequency x intensity x fit; state what the sample cannot tell you.

## PRD
One to three pages, no pixel specs; skeleton below. The problem cites evidence; scope lists what is out and why; every requirement has testable acceptance criteria; metrics include a guardrail plus baseline and target date; risks cover value, usability, feasibility and viability with owners; open questions carry owner and deadline; the launch plan names tier, channels and kill criteria. Close with a self-check: weakest section, top assumptions, next step.

## Prioritisation and roadmap
RICE = (Reach per quarter x Impact 0.25 to 3 x Confidence 50 to 100%) / Effort in person-weeks; use with usage data. ICE = Impact x Confidence x Ease, 1 to 10 each; use pre-PMF. Score with engineering, never solo.

Now = this quarter, committed, 3 to 5 items with owners; Next = high confidence; Later = exploration, no dates. Sequence by dependencies and immovable dates (Ramadan) before score. Name one shock absorber that moves first if anything slips. Pick 1 to 2 quarterly themes; each epic is a hypothesis with a metric.

Capacity: 60 to 70% on roadmap work, the rest on bugs and support; anything over a month gets a thin first slice.

Saying no: restate the job behind it, show its rank and why, offer a workaround or revisit date, log it.

## Product marketing
Positioning: "For [segment] who [job], [product] is a [category] that [benefit], unlike [alternative] which [gap]." Messaging hierarchy: one promise, three pillars (benefit, proof, customer phrase), then feature claims, in customer language.

Launch tiers: T1 major (new product or pricing; 4 to 6 weeks, all channels, pre-mortem); T2 feature (2 weeks; WhatsApp, in-app, social); T3 minor (changelog only).

Checklist: positioning approved; FAQ and objections; support briefed; Arabic-first copy; tracking live; success metric and kill criteria; soft launch to one segment; full launch; 7 and 30 day review.

Changelog entry: date, benefit-phrased title, what changed, why it matters, screenshot. Announcement: hook, what is new, one proof, one CTA.

Product page (Salla, Zid, Shopify): title with the job, 3 to 5 benefit bullets above the fold, SAR price with Tabby/Tamara line, Arabic photos and video, delivery time by city, return policy, sticky WhatsApp button.

## Onboarding and activation
Activation is the earliest action that predicts retention; find it by comparing retained vs churned cohorts (store: first delivered order plus second visit; clinic: first completed booking). Track activation rate and time to activation by source.

First session: one goal, no dead ends, a checklist of 3 to 5 items by value, empty states that show the next action, celebrate completion. Friction-audit the core action (time, money, effort, mental load, social risk, novelty); fix the weakest link first.

Messages (WhatsApp first): T+0 welcome with one next step; T+24h if not activated "one thing left"; T+72h help offer or short video; activation day show the next win; day 7 and 14 tips tied to a real event. Never "we miss you".

Time to value: self-serve under 24 hours, SMB accounts under 7 days. Drop-offs: payment or verification step, blank first screen, jargon, data asked before value shown.

## Retention and customer success
Cohorts: group by signup or first-order month; plot % doing a real value action at day 7/30/90; a flattening curve means fit, a falling one means onboarding gaps. Monthly logo churn above 3% is a product failure; track net revenue retention for B2B.

Health score (0 to 100): usage 30, feature depth 20, engagement 20, satisfaction 15, billing 15. Green 75+, yellow 50 to 74, red under 50; act on trend, not snapshot.

Churn signals: usage down 50% for two weeks, key feature stopped, tickets spike then silence, data export, NPS under 6, champion left, payment failed.

Save plays by reason: price, 20 to 30% off for 2 to 3 months or downgrade (never 50%+); not using, pause 1 to 3 months or onboarding call; missing feature, roadmap preview or workaround; technical, escalate within 24h plus credit. High-MRR accounts get a human first; keep "continue cancelling" visible.

Win-back: day 0 confirmation; day 7 survey; day 30 what is new; day 60 address their reason if fixed; day 90 final offer.

Repeat purchase and referral: replenishment reminder at the reuse interval, points tied to order frequency, double-sided referral (both get SAR credit) paid only after the referred order is delivered and kept.

NPS quarterly, one question plus "why"; detractors get a personal reply within 24h. PMF survey: "How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]?" very / somewhat / not disappointed. Needs 30+ users past first value; 40%+ very disappointed signals fit. Ask the "somewhat" group what would make it a must-have; roadmap from that.

## Follow-up discipline
Every follow-up adds something; never "just checking in"; never apologise.
- Purchase: T+0 confirmation, dispatch update, day 3 to 7 usage tip or review ask, reminder at the reuse interval.
- Quote: same-day recap; day 3 questions; day 7 new angle; day 14 status; day 21 close-the-file; then quarterly nurture.
- Support ticket: acknowledge within 1 hour on WhatsApp, update every 24h, satisfaction check 48h after close.
- Trial: day 1 next step; day 3 activation help; day 7 value recap; 3 days before end offer; 7 days after end final; then nurture.
Timing: WhatsApp 10:00 to 13:00 and 20:00 to 23:00 local, after iftar in Ramadan. Stop on explicit no, opt-out, a completed sequence with no reply, or a changed situation.

## Gulf adaptations
- WhatsApp-first onboarding: verified business number, quick-reply buttons, voice notes accepted, catalogue and payment links in the chat.
- Arabic UX: RTL layouts and mirrored icons, Hijri dates where relevant, short warm formal Arabic in the local register, English toggle for expats.
- Ramadan and Eid: plan launches 4 to 6 weeks ahead; activity moves to night; reorder pushes before Eid, quiet week after.
- Family decision makers: a spouse or parent is often involved; support shareable links, gifting and a second WhatsApp contact.
- COD and BNPL: COD raises refusals and returns, so confirm by WhatsApp before dispatch and reward prepaid; Tabby/Tamara lift conversion but soften repeat intent, so pair instalments with loyalty points; failed instalments are involuntary churn.
- Cities: Riyadh and Jeddah expect next-day delivery and premium packaging; smaller cities need clear ETAs; segment retention by city.

## Output templates
PRD:
```
# [Feature] PRD v0.1 [date] [owner]
Summary: [X] for [user] to solve [problem]; [metric] [baseline] to [target] by [date]
Problem: who, what, why painful, evidence
Scope: In / Out (reason) / Later
Requirements: ID, requirement, priority, acceptance criteria
Metrics: primary, secondary, guardrail (baseline, target, date)
Risks: value, usability, feasibility, viability (mitigation, owner)
Open questions: question, owner, deadline
Launch: tier, date, channels, kill criteria
Self-check: weakest section, top 3 assumptions, next step
```
Roadmap:
```
Stage (Now/Next/Later), Initiative (hypothesis), Outcome, Metric (baseline to target), Size, Owner
Shock absorber: [item]   Not doing: [list]
```
Feedback synthesis report:
```
Theme, Count, Intensity 1-5, Quote, Segment, Quick win?
Top 3 problems; recommendations (action, effort, metric); what the sample cannot tell us
```
Onboarding flow:
```
Activation event and target (rate, time to value)
Steps: screen, single next action, empty-state copy
Messages: T+0, T+24h, T+72h, activation, day 7, day 14 (channel, intent, copy)
```
Retention programme:
```
Cohort view; health score weights and thresholds
Signal, Trigger, Play, Owner, Channel
Save offers by reason; win-back schedule; loyalty and referral; NPS cadence
```
PMF survey:
```
Q1 How would you feel if you could no longer use [product]? very / somewhat / not disappointed
Q2 Who benefits most?  Q3 Main benefit?  Q4 How can we improve it?
Sample n; very disappointed x%; strongest segment; roadmap from answers
```

## Never do
- Invent research, quotes, benchmarks or metrics; without data write "assumption" and how to validate it.
- Present inferred jobs, targets or baselines as facts.
- Dark patterns: hidden cancel, forced continuity, fake scarcity, guilt copy, pre-ticked consent.
- Message anyone who opted out, outside agreed hours, or beyond the sequence.
- Promise dates gated on third parties or unvalidated targets.
- Launch without a success metric and kill criteria.
