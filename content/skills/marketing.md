# Marketing content playbook (Noor)

## Principles
- The task spec and brand file win over this playbook: counts, lengths, channels and limits come from the task.
- One piece, one reader, one idea, one ask.
- Write the CTA first; every line leads to it. Default Gulf ask: a WhatsApp message. If the brand file has no WhatsApp number or link, write [رابط واتساب], never a bare "راسلنا".
- Specific beats adjectives: a sourced fact or figure, a scene, a sourced district (else [الحي]). Cut "best", "unique", "exceptional" unless proof follows.
- Customer words over company words; "you" outnumbers "we" 2 to 1.
- The hook earns the next line, the body earns belief, the close earns the action.
- Evidence rule: every price, discount, date, count, story and claim is sourced (brand file, owner notes or given workspace data) or computed from them (price ÷ days of use only with sourced days). CART DATA gives product names only: never cart counts, values or that shopper data was read, in any item. Anything else is [رقم] / [number] (optionally labelled, e.g. "(owner to set)") or a labelled assumption ("افتراض"). A proof line (review, customer count, story) with no source is left out of customer copy, never a placeholder; in the weekly plan it is [دليل مطلوب / proof needed]. Facts stay apart from projections; an estimate is never a result.
- Goals are outputs: pieces drafted, approved and published, never outcomes.
- Every item is complete and paste-ready: no notes, options or explanations inside.

## Content strategy and pillars
Pick 3 to 5 pillars from what the business sells, questions customers ask before buying, and objections heard on WhatsApp. Each piece is searchable or shareable, with one funnel stage: awareness (ما هو، كيف), consideration (أفضل، مقارنة، تجربة), decision (سعر، حجز، عرض), retention (use, care, reorder).
Post types to rotate: tip (a mistake, then the fix), story (moment, tension, lesson; sourced), proof (a sourced review or count, or a demonstration), question or take, behind the scenes, offer.
Weekly mix: 3 helpful, 2 proof (if none is sourced: demonstration or behind the scenes, never an invented story or review), 1 community or seasonal, 1 direct offer; no pillar two days running, never two offers back to back; anchor the week on any season in the live Gulf calendar.
Repurpose one idea a week into atoms (a tip, a fact, an objection answered), each rewritten to stand alone on another channel.

## Hooks and copy structure
Match the hook to awareness. Cold reader: name the problem or identity in their words. Knows the category: lead with the difference. Ready to buy: product, offer, where, a deadline only if sourced. Benefit beats news beats pure curiosity; combine them.
Draft 3 to 5 hooks from different families, ship only the most specific: an illustrative scene or a sourced one-line story (never an invented anecdote told as real); a common mistake; "stop X, do Y"; before and after (only if sourced); a self-interest question; sourced news (branch, item, hours); a sourced figure plus what it means.
Body: one order per piece. PAS for cold readers in pain, before-after-bridge for transformation (the after is what the product does, sourced, never a promised personal result), promise-picture-proof-push for offers. Drill each feature: what it is, so you can, which means. Name the pain once, then relieve it. Paragraphs of 1 to 3 lines.
Proof comes after desire, next to the ask. Adjectives prove nothing; product facts, sourced reviews and demonstration do. An outcome the brief cannot back becomes what the product does (mechanism, ingredient, step), never a hedged effect ("غالباً"، "في أغلب الحالات").
Close: what to do, how, why now. Open with a doable verb (احجز، جرّب، راسلنا، اطلب). One offer, one ask. Urgency needs a sourced date or quantity; otherwise a reason to act without time pressure (an easy first step), never an invented cost of waiting.
Headline: write as if it is the only line read: product and its benefit in it, audience flagged ("لأصحاب المطاعم"), facts over cleverness, the product as hero. Final test: swap in a competitor's name; if the copy still fits, rewrite.

## Persuasion used honestly
Use: social proof (sourced counts, reviews, names); reciprocity (a useful tip before the ask); loss framing ("لا تفوّتك"); scarcity only with a sourced limit or date; anchoring on a sourced previous price; sourced authority (license, founding year), else none; a small first step (a WhatsApp question before buying); price per day or per use (sourced divisor); admitting one limit to gain trust.
The line: no fake scarcity, countdowns or reviews, no invented before and after, no shame, no fear about health or family. A lever never replaces a real reason to buy.

## Platform craft
Instagram: the hook sits in the first 125 characters before the cut. Caption 60 to 150 words: hook, value in short lines, one CTA. Ask for a save, share, DM or WhatsApp message, not a like. Hashtag count as the task asks, otherwise 3 to 5.
Carousel: slide 1 hooks alone; per slide one idea, one short line, a visual direction; arc: hook, problem, why it matters, fix, product or proof, CTA.
Snapchat: 4 to 6 snaps under 10 seconds, one idea each, spoken dialect to camera. Snap 1 hooks, the middle shows the product in use, the last carries the swipe-up or WhatsApp CTA. Per snap: the spoken line plus on-screen text of 6 words or fewer.
TikTok and Reels: inside the task's time cap, otherwise 15 to 30 seconds. Hook in the first 2 seconds, spoken and on screen, opening on a close-up or the result. Then beats of [VO], [VISUAL], [TEXT], one shot each, hard cuts; cut any beat the story survives without. The ending pays off the hook. Text clear of edge app buttons, captions burned in, commercial-library music only. Caption with the phrase people search, 3 to 5 hashtags.
X: 280 characters, one idea, first line is the hook. Gulf X is news and opinion: be direct. 1 or 2 hashtags; links go in a reply.
WhatsApp Business: 700 characters or fewer, one purpose. Line 1 greets with {{1}}, names the brand and why they receive this; then value, one CTA (reply word or button), then the opt-out if due: "للإلغاء أرسل: إيقاف". Variables numbered in order, never first or last in the body, never adjacent.
Google Business Profile: 300 characters or fewer; the first 80 carry the news or offer. Name service and district naturally ("[الخدمة] في حي [الحي]"). One CTA matching the button (Call, Book, Order, Learn more). No hashtags, no phone number in the text.

## Paid ad copy
Start with 3 to 5 angles, not rewordings: pain, outcome, proof, identity ("لأهل جدة"), offer, old way versus new (outcome = what the customer gets, sourced, never a guaranteed or quantified result); 2 to 3 variations each, labelled with the angle, one variable changed per variation. Spread headlines across benefit, feature plus benefit, offer or proof (sourced only), place or identity, action-led. Each headline must read right beside any primary text.
Limits: Meta hook within 125 characters, headline 40, description 30; TikTok text 80 to 100; Snapchat headline 34. Count beside each capped field's label.
Headlines: specific benefit, active verb, sourced numbers. Message match: the page or WhatsApp prefilled line after the click repeats the ad's promise, offer and price, and the button names that step; no matching page means WhatsApp, not the homepage. Audience note: who (age, city, interest), awareness level, why this angle fits. Daily budget: the client's figure or a labelled assumption, in SAR unless the brand's market differs, never paired with promised results.

## SEO article craft
Match the format to search intent: informational (guide, steps), commercial (comparison, criteria table), transactional (service page: price, area, booking), local (city plus service). One primary long-tail keyword competitors likely miss and the site does not already target. Never state search volume or promise a ranking.
Structure: title, meta, one H1 with the keyword, a 2 to 3 sentence answer first, H2s that mirror real questions with H3 steps, a table or numbered list where it helps, a local section (city, districts, season), FAQ, CTA. Cover only what the business can credibly speak to; no competitor claims.
Title 50 to 60 characters, keyword near the front, brand last. Meta within the task cap (130 to 150 characters by default), one specific benefit, ends in a CTA. Keyword in title, H1, first 100 words and meta, spelled as people type it, city included; then 5 to 8 related terms, density under 2%.
3 to 5 internal links with descriptive anchors to pages that exist; otherwise [رابط داخلي: الموضوع]. FAQ: the task's count (otherwise 4 to 6), the searcher's words, answers of 40 to 60 words. E-E-A-T: first-hand brand-file details, a last-updated date (TODAY, else [date]), a source only when the brief gives one (never "دراسات تثبت"), a byline only when the brand file names the author, never an inline [name] placeholder.

## Message sequences (WhatsApp and cart recovery)
Plan it silently; deliver only the messages the task asks for, timing and intent in each title:
- Trigger: event (cart left, lead gone quiet, order delivered) and delay from the task.
- Segment: opted-in contacts; exclude buyers since the trigger, opt-outs, blocked or reported numbers, other sequences.
- Per message: timing, intent, CTA; each adds a new reason to act, no repeated text. Exit on purchase, a reply, opt-out, failed delivery.
Lifecycle: a buyer never gets a cart reminder, a lead never gets a review request, the thank-you carries one ask (the review), not an offer; order and delivery messages never turn into promotions.
Cart arc, every CTA to {{2}}: 1 = helpful reminder, questions welcome; 2 = answer the likely hesitation; 3 = gentle last reminder with the opt-out. Incentive only from current offers.
Hesitations, brand file only: price → value per use or listed payment options (mada, Apple Pay, Tabby, Tamara, cash on delivery); size → size guide, exchange; delivery → areas, times; returns → the policy; authenticity → warranty, official dealer, origin.
Consent: recorded (when, where); opt-out on every standalone marketing message; in a sequence, on the touch the task spec names (the third cart reminder), else on every touch; low frequency. Business WhatsApp and SMS go out between 9:00 and 22:00 local time, never at prayer times or Friday midday; in Ramadan shift later (after iftar and taraweeh). Blocks and reports lower the number's quality rating: relevance beats volume.

## Arabic and dialect craft
Light "white" dialect for Instagram, Snapchat, TikTok, X and friendly WhatsApp. Simplified MSA for articles, GBP, LinkedIn, formal WhatsApp, legal, medical and B2B. One dialect per piece.
- Negate with ما: "ما تفوّتك". Not "لن تفوتك" or "لم نجد".
- The verb stands alone: "تندم", "نوصّل لك". Never Levantine or Egyptian helpers: "رح تندم", "عم نجهّز", "بتحب", "هتلاقي".
- Everyday words: شوف، يسوّي، أبي، الحين، عشان. Not انظر، يفعل، أريد، الآن، لكي.
- Saudi: وش تبي، مرة حلو، يجنن. Najdi is firmer: زين، أبا. Hejazi is softer: إيش، دحين.
- Emirati: شو، وايد، مب ("مب غالي"). Kuwaiti: شنو، وايد، مو ("شنو تنطر؟").
- Hooks drop the pronoun: "تدور مطعم عائلي؟" not "هل أنت تبحث عن".
Voice check: read it aloud as the owner talking to a regular customer. Off → on: "نحن متحمسون للإعلان عن" → "وصل الجديد"; "لا تتردد في التواصل معنا" → "راسلنا واتساب"; "أفضل جودة وأسعار منافسة" → one checkable brand-file fact. Cut AI tells: نقلة نوعية، حلول مبتكرة، تجربة استثنائية، الخبراء يقولون; stacked "مو مجرد كذا، هو كذا"; three rhetorical questions in a row; ellipses on every line.
Numbers: Western digits, one digit system per piece. Currency after the number: "[رقم] ريال" on social, ر.س or SAR in ads and formal text, درهم or AED, د.ك or KWD with three decimals. A stated price says whether VAT is included (where levied: KSA 15%, UAE 5%, Bahrain 10%) only as the brand file confirms; otherwise [شامل الضريبة؟].
Hashtags: Arabic words joined by underscores. Mix one brand tag, Arabic category or city tags (#مطاعم_الرياض) and at least one English (#RiyadhEats); the total follows the task.

## Gulf adaptations
- Rhythm: the week runs Sunday to Thursday in KSA and Kuwait, Monday to Friday in the UAE. Evenings after Isha are the usual strong slot (check account insights). Never schedule on a prayer time, above all Friday midday. KSA government pay lands around the 27th; private payroll varies. Place payday offers accordingly.
- Seasonal prep starts 4 to 6 weeks ahead (6 to 8 for a full launch). Ramadan: attention moves to after iftar and late night until suhoor. Generous, calm tone; sell softly in the last ten nights. Eid: greeting first, then gifting offers.
- National days: Saudi Founding Day 22 Feb and National Day 23 Sep, Kuwait 25 and 26 Feb, UAE 2 Dec, Bahrain 16 Dec, Qatar 18 Dec. No flag, emblem or leaders' images in promotions. Late November is White Friday, not Black Friday. Back to school (late Aug to Sep): supplies, uniforms, routines; offers before term.
- Modesty: conservative visuals and wording, no alcohol, gambling or dating references, no Quran verses used to sell, no jokes on religion, tribe or nationality.
- Regulated claims (the client confirms licenses). Health: no cure, guarantee or before and after promise. Legal: never promise outcomes. Real estate: show "رقم ترخيص الإعلان: [رقم]", no guaranteed returns. Finance: no guaranteed profit. KSA discounts need a Ministry of Commerce license: state one only when the client supplies it.

## Output templates
Weekly plan row: Day and date | Local time (suggested) | Channel and format | Pillar, post type, stage | Post idea and hook | CTA. Then 2 ad ideas (channel, angle, audience) and 1 WhatsApp idea (trigger, segment, purpose). Goal line: pieces to draft, approve and publish.
Ad: Angle | Primary text | Headline (count) | Description or button | Audience note | Daily budget.
GBP post: Type (offer, update, event) | Text | CTA button. Offer dates and terms from the brand file, else [date].

## Never do
- Never invent prices, discounts, statistics, certifications, testimonials, guarantees or deadlines. Unknown numbers become [رقم] / [number].
- Never promise or forecast results (sales, leads, followers, rankings, ROI; in health, legal, finance or real estate, no outcome at all), state a benchmark as fact, or name or attack a competitor.
- Never put meta commentary, options or apologies inside a deliverable, or deliver a partial item.
- Never mix dialects, use Levantine or Egyptian forms for a Gulf audience, or ship translated-English Arabic.
- Never use fake urgency, fake scarcity, engagement bait or clickbait.
- Never exceed a platform limit, link to pages that do not exist, or write a real link where a placeholder like {{2}} is required.
- Never message contacts who did not opt in, or omit a due opt-out line.
